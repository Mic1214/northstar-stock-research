BUILD YOUR KNOWLEDGE

Learning Center

Short explanations of concepts used in stock research.

01 / BASICS

Stock

A stock represents an ownership share in a company. Owning a share does not guarantee a profit; its market price can rise or fall.

Question to remember

What part of the business do I own, and what could make that ownership more or less valuable?

02 / FINANCIALS

Revenue vs. profit

Revenue is money earned from selling goods or services before expenses. Profit is what remains after the relevant expenses are deducted.

Question to remember

Is the company growing sales while also managing its costs?

03 / VALUATION

P/E ratio

The price-to-earnings ratio compares a share price with earnings per share. It can help compare valuations, but it is less useful when earnings are negative or unusually high or low.

Question to remember

What growth and risk expectations might explain this valuation?

04 / RISK

Diversification

Diversification means spreading investments across different holdings so the result depends less on any single one. It can reduce some risks, but it cannot eliminate market risk.

Question to remember

Do my holdings depend on the same industry, event, or economic factor?

For formal financial definitions and deeper study, use reputable educational sources and primary documents. Source links will be added as the research features are built.
